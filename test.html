Chocolate Cake Recipe

This page contains a simple recipe for making a chocolate cake. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, eggs, and butter, then bake at 180°C.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
